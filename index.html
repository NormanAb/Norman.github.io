Self-Balancing 2-Axis Rope-Suspended System

Team of 2 · Summer 2026
  • Built a self-balancing, rope-suspended wheel that stabilizes across two axes using an IMU, stepper motor, and Li-Po battery pack.
  • Implemented PID control in Arduino C++ to hold the chassis level under disturbances like rope sway and wind.
  • Sized circuit protection to each load (0.5 A for the Arduino, 2 A for the battery) and hand-crimped wiring of varying gauges to integrate the motor, driver, and battery.
  • Tuning the PID was the hardest part — early attempts caused jitters and higher current draw before the gains were dialed in.
NEMA17 stepper · Arduino Uno R3 · MPU6050 IMU · 1000mAh Li-Po · DRV8825 driver · 0.5A / 2A fuses

Solar Panel Tracking System

Team of 2 · Summer 2026
  • Used light-dependent resistors (LDRs) to sense the light source's position and steered the panel toward it with servos.
  • Designed a dual power-supply architecture, isolating the higher-draw servo circuit from the Arduino and its logic-level wiring.
  • Increases sun exposure throughout the day compared to a fixed panel.
  • Main challenge was calibrating the LDRs to respond consistently to the same light and translating that signal into servo movement.
Breadboard · Arduino Uno · LDR modules · 180° servo motors · AA battery holder
